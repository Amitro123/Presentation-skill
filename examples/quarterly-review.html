Highlights

Three things that moved the numbers

Out of eleven launches, these had the largest measurable effect.

Self-serve onboarding cut time to first value from 9 days to 3.
The new export API removed the top support request of the quarter.
Weekly release trains replaced ad-hoc deploys across all teams.
Smaller, more frequent releases gave us earlier feedback at lower risk.
